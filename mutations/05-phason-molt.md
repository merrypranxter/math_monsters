# 05 — THE PHASON MOLT

## Surgery map

- **Governing mathematics:** quasiperiodic tiling with local matching rules and phason flips.
- **Body:** interlocking living plates with long-range order but no translational repetition.
- **Eyes:** temporary defects where matching rules cannot be satisfied.
- **Mouths:** zipper-like chains of phason strain, never arbitrary holes.
- **Teeth:** legal local tile configurations that become illegal as the body flips.
- **Reused organs:** black keylines, CMYK bootleg color, diatom-like armor, stop-motion inserts, hydraulic pressure, recursive chroma bleed.
- **New metabolism:** the creature moves by changing which local arrangement counts as legal.

## Master still-image prompt

```text
A lost 1988 underground fantasy-science-fiction body-horror frame: a room-filling quasiperiodic organism built as an elaborate physical puppet of interlocking foam-latex plates. Its anatomy is generated by a genuine aperiodic matching system with long-range order and no repeating unit. It must not resemble a normal animal covered in Penrose decoration. The shifting plates are the body.

Hundreds of wet, slightly translucent rubber cells interlock through two families of rhombic and kite-like local relationships, reinforced by thick glossy black industrial-rubber boundaries. The cells never settle into an ordinary grid, bilateral symmetry, or repeating wallpaper. Pneumatic phason flips mechanically exchange one legal local arrangement for another. Realistic human eyes hatch only at temporary matching-rule defects where the surrounding cells cannot reconcile their edge conditions: wet sclera, articulated lids shaped by the defect boundary, halftone-dotted irises, sharp prismatic tear-film highlights. Complete functional human mouths open only along extended chains of accumulated phason strain. Their lips unzip cell by cell, exposing gums, tongues, and teeth whose packing is legal on one side of the flip and impossible on the other. When the local configuration repairs itself, the eye or mouth is physically squeezed flat and redistributed into the tiling.

The practical prop uses individually cast foam-latex plates on ratcheting tracks, pneumatic pockets, replacement defect appliances, cable-operated eyes, segmented mechanical jaws, stop-motion phason insert shots, black rubber keylines, translucent silicone underlayers, and a concealed hydraulic compression table. Surface color collides flat underground offset-print intensity with real wet optical behavior: aggressive magenta, electric cyan, highlighter yellow, toxic chartreuse, ultraviolet violet, occasional tangerine, mica-laced glycerin and prismatic white highlights, all separated by deep black structural boundaries. Hard frontal light holds the keylines while saturated tungsten backlight glows through the cells.

Oblique static wide shot, as if the camera has caught a portion of an organism continuing beyond all four frame edges. No central face. One defect eye is large in the near field; a phason-mouth chain crosses diagonally through the middle; smaller defects accumulate toward the distance without simple recursion. Pushed 16mm, optical stop-motion generations, consumer VHS copy deteriorated in attic heat. Analog CMYK misregistration follows the quasiperiodic matching directions, not a generic RGB split. Chroma bleed momentarily creates illegal color neighbors, matte lines shimmer around replacement cells, mild tracking instability triggers apparent defect migration, bottom-edge dropout erases several legal matches. Tactile, saturated, joyful, disturbing, mechanically possible and mathematically governed.
```
