# 04 — THE ULTRAMETRIC NURSERY

## Surgery map

- **Governing mathematics:** p-adic / ultrametric distance, where anatomical closeness is determined by shared recursive ancestry rather than physical proximity.
- **Body:** several differently scaled practical sets that are one organism because their internal address matches.
- **Eyes:** nested cylinders sharing a p-adic prefix focus and blink together across the frame.
- **Mouths:** family classes of recursive mouths; a tiny distant mouth may be closer to a giant foreground mouth than to its physical neighbor.
- **Tongues:** pass between scale models through optical-printer mattes.
- **Reused organs:** recursive teeth, distributed life, scale instability, replacement appliances, multiple exposure, prismatic eyes, chroma cascade.
- **New metabolism:** it digests by inheritance. Anatomy acts according to its address, not its location.

## Master still-image prompt

```text
A densely layered frame from a supposedly lost 1985 metaphysical creature film, assembled entirely from physical puppet sections and period optical compositing. The visible organism obeys an ultrametric p-adic anatomy: spatial distance is irrelevant. Body parts are close only when they share the same recursive address. Do not depict a literal branching tree, diagram, grid, or mathematical symbols.

The foreground contains one enormous working human mouth built at full scale from wet foam latex, articulated lips, gums, tongue, and imperfect dental acrylic teeth. Inside the folds beside it are clusters of successively smaller complete mouths, but the recursion is not a simple mouth-inside-mouth tunnel. Across distant corners and different depth planes, other mouths of radically different physical sizes repeat the same precise lip crease, missing tooth, and tongue motion because they share a p-adic prefix. A tiny mouth at the far edge opens in exact synchronization with the huge foreground mouth while the apparently adjacent mouth remains still. Numerous realistic human eyes appear in nested scale classes: full-size acrylic eye mechanisms, miniature replacement eyes, and optical micro-inserts. Eyes sharing ancestry have identical iris defects and blink together across impossible distances; neighboring eyes with different addresses ignore one another. Tongues disappear through black optical mattes and emerge from related mouths in separate scale models. The anatomy is a hierarchy of kinship without a visible tree.

The practical production consists of six differently scaled foam-latex sets photographed separately: an oversized mouth landscape, a tabletop colony, miniature rubber folds, macro dental inserts, mechanical acrylic eyes, rod-puppeted tongues, mirrors, forced perspective, traveling mattes, and repeated optical-printer passes. The joins are convincing but retain faint grain mismatch and glowing matte edges. Aggressive spectral materiality remains coherent across scale: opalescent electric cyan tissue, ultraviolet-violet cavities, hot-pink lips, acid-lime saliva, cobalt shadows, prismatic white eye highlights, deep black address gaps. The same material defect repeats at corresponding p-adic scales like an anatomical checksum.

Static wide frame composed as an impossible optical collage rather than a centered monster. Each scale pass has slightly different depth of field and film grain, making scale itself unstable. Shot on 16mm and repeatedly rephotographed before VHS transfer. Attic damage acts ultrametrically: related anatomy shares the same chroma error regardless of screen position. A magenta ghost blooms simultaneously around every mouth in one ancestry class; cyan delay infects a separate eye family; brief RF dropout removes an entire recursive branch across multiple disconnected areas. Ultra-realistic rubber, glycerin, acrylic, rods, matte seams, optical-generation loss, telecine softness, tape flutter. The viewer can see how every section was built but cannot determine which parts are near each other.
```

## Image-to-video prompt

```text
Locked static optical-collage composition; never zoom into a recursive tunnel. Animate by ultrametric kinship instead of screen position. The giant foreground mouth, a thumbnail-size upper-corner mouth, and three microscopic macro inserts share one p-adic address and therefore inhale, bite, lick, and swallow in exact synchronization. Physically adjacent mouths belonging to other address classes move on different quasiperiodic cycles. Eyes with matching iris defects blink and focus together across separate scale passes. Rod-puppeted tongues enter black traveling mattes and emerge from related mouths elsewhere. Replacement appliances create abrupt but physical scale transitions; no digital interpolation. Let each scale layer retain its own film grain, depth of field, cable hesitation, saliva behavior, and mechanical timing. VHS damage also follows ancestry: one family acquires magenta chroma ghosts, another cyan edge delay, another momentarily disappears in an RF dropout. Preserve optical matte shimmer, repeated-generation softness, gate weave, attic tape flutter, and saturated opalescent color.
```
