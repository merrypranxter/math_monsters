# Math Monsters: Forbidden Biological Glamour

Twenty self-contained still-image prompts. Every composition is generated by a new mathematical mechanism and contains only flesh, realistic human eyes, and functional human mouths. Beauty comes from geometry, color, material, and light—not from a human figure.
