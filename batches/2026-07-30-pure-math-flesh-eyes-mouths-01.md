# Math Monsters: Pure Math / Flesh / Eyes / Mouths

Twenty self-contained master still-image prompts. Every image contains only an abstract mathematical organism made from wet flesh, realistic human eyes, and functional human mouths. The mathematical system generates the anatomy, composition, repetition, scale, and placement of every feature.

## 1 — The Mandelbulb Flesh Bloom

```text
An extreme macro still from a lost 1986 psychedelic practical-effects film: a single frame-filling Mandelbulb organism constructed entirely from wet living flesh, realistic human eyes, and functional human mouths. No head, torso, limbs, clothing, scenery, props, symbols, or written mathematics. The global body is unmistakably a three-dimensional Mandelbulb: eightfold radial lobes of glossy pearlescent-white and coral-pink tissue erupt into smaller copies of the complete organism, which divide again into miniature lobes, mouths, eyelids, and tongues until the recursion disappears beneath the resolution of the film. Every bulb generated by the iterative formula contains one hyper-real human eye at its stable interior center, with wet sclera, turquoise or amber fibrous irises, dark pupils, capillaries, soft lids, and sharp tear-film reflections. Wherever the iteration escapes toward infinity, the boundary tears open into a realistic mouth with saturated hot-pink lips, pale gums, individual ivory teeth, saliva threads, and a thick tongue curling into the next recursive bulb. Large mouths dominate the nearest lobes while hundreds of smaller mouths repeat deeper into the structure.

The mathematical form itself fills every edge of the composition; the only visible background is tiny deep-cobalt negative space trapped between recursive flesh branches. Build the hero surface from glossy silicone and foam latex, with nested miniature sculptures optically composited into the larger lobes so the recursion remains tactile, irregular, and handmade rather than digitally clean. Internal turquoise, ultraviolet, and acid-lime light passes through thinner membranes; tangerine edge light and hard white pin-spots reveal pores, tears, gums, and saliva. The palette is pearlescent white, coral pink, hot pink, turquoise, cobalt, acid lime, ultraviolet violet, and tangerine, all physically present in tissue or transmitted light. Shot static on 35mm with shallow focus and dense foreground overlap, then transferred through Betacam to a colorful attic-damaged VHS: gentle chroma bleed doubles the smallest eyes, pink halation blooms around lips, and faint tracking noise bends only the finest recursion. The image is nothing except infinite mathematical flesh watching and eating itself.
```

## 2 — The Möbius Mouth Ribbon

```text

Glossy hot-pink lips run uninterrupted around the organism, sometimes tightly closed, sometimes opening into dozens of connected oral chambers with realistic gums, individual pearl teeth, wet tongues, and saliva filaments. Because the edge is one loop, every apparent mouth is visibly part of the same endless mouth; a tongue emerging in the foreground can be followed around the twist until it reappears upside down in the background.
